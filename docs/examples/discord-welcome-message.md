**Welcome to Neural Network Games.**

This server is where neural networks learn to play Game Boy games - trained, measured, and watched the whole way, no hand-holding once a run starts.

Start here:
- {{rules}} - the handful of rules that keep this good, read once
- {{general}} - say hello, everyone's here
- {{help}} - stuck getting set up or reproducing a result? ask here
- {{agentgb}} - the network that plays: training runs, certified results, failures
- {{terminalgb}} - the emulator itself: accuracy work, test-suite scores
- {{research-discussion}} - method, training, reward shaping, thinking out loud
- {{announcements}} - milestones and releases, low volume

You've been given the Trainer role to start.
From there you climb through the eight gym badges as you get involved - watch for them beside names in the member list.

Glad you're here.
